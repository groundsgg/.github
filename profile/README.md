We're building **Grounds**, a Minecraft minigame network where competitive play, exploration, and a shared world belong together. A place to find your next match, meet friends, and become part of a city with a story of its own.

### A city worth coming back to

Our world begins in an optimistic industrial city: stone architecture, copper pipes, busy workshops, bridges, and machines built for a purpose. Beneath it lies **Vaerith**, a mineral that stores energy. Refined into protected cores, it powers the city's machinery. Its energy is familiar to the people who work with it; where that energy comes from remains a mystery.

You arrive as a traveler and find your own place. Get to know the residents, explore side streets and maintenance passages, or help bring a city project to life. Seasons will introduce discoveries, open new districts, and leave lasting traces of earlier chapters.

The city is designed to grow with its community, giving every return from a match somewhere to belong.

### Your next match. Your own pace.

Our starting lineup centers on **SurvivalGames, BedWars, and kit-based 1vs1**. Each has a place in the city, with walkable meeting points and a direct game selector when you're ready to queue.

Story, conversations, and exploration are optional. Core game modes stay accessible from the start, and you can pick up the story whenever you feel like it. Play another round, practice with friends, or spend a little longer in the city.

### Built behind the scenes

This organization brings together the games and the platform that supports them:

- **Games and worlds** — minigames, shared gameplay APIs, scenes, NPCs, and resource packs for Paper and Minestom.
- **One connected network** — player identity, friends, parties, chat, matchmaking, and moderation across servers and proxies.
- **Tools for the team** — the Grounds CLI, Gradle integrations, Forge, and Portal take plugins and services from local development into managed test and deployment environments.
- **Infrastructure** — Kubernetes, Agones, maintained containers, and shared services provide the foundation for running it all.

We're building the player experience and its tools together, so the technology serves the worlds and games we want to create.
